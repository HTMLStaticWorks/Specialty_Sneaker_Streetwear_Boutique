How We Verify Every Detail

Our specialists look far beyond surface aesthetics. Counterfeits have improved, but precision engineering and genuine factory standards leave unmistakable footprints:

  • UV Blacklight Analysis: Reveals invisible factory stampings, guideline glue trails, and correct stitch luminescence.
  • RFID & Barcode Decoding: Scans manufacturer chips and box label font weights against original brand databases.
  • Material Density & Weight: High-precision gram scales verify exact rubber compounding and leather densities.
  • Scent & Chemical Profile: Genuine deadstock factory solvent aroma distinguishes retail pairs from rep adhesives.

The 4 Pillars of Verification

Every single product on our shelves must pass all 4 checkpoints without exception.

1. Box & Label

Font kerning, barcode SKU alignment, RFID chip frequency, and box cardboard texture analysis.

2. Construction

Stitch pitch counts per inch, midsole pattern precision, toe box perforation, and inner footbed stitching.

3. UV & Spectral

Under 365nm blacklight, factory stamp inks, thread luminescence, and hidden guidelines are exposed.

4. NFC Sealed

Secured with our serialized tamper-proof NFC badge linking directly to its digital authenticity record.
