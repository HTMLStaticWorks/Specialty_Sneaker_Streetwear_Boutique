How to Buy

Everything you need to know before shopping at GRAILS Boutique.

In-Store Shopping

Visit us in-store to see, try, and feel every pair before you buy. Our staff are collectors themselves — we'll help you find your perfect pair.

Online Reservations

See something you want? Reserve it online and collect in-store or choose shipping. Payment confirmed on collection or at checkout.

Drop Raffles

For limited drops, we run a fair raffle. Sign up via our site, check your email for results, and collect your W pair in-store.

Authentication Process

  1. Each pair is submitted for authentication upon arrival at GRAILS.
  2. Our expert authenticators inspect stitching, materials, labelling, and sole units.
  3. Authenticated pairs receive a GRAILS verification card and protective packaging.

For consignment inquiries, bring your pair to the store. We authenticate on-site and list within 48 hours if accepted.

Ready to Shop?

Come in-store or browse our available stock online. New inventory drops every week.
