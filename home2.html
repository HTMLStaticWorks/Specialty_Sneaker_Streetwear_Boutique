Microscopic Forensics

The GRAILS Verification Lab

Zero fakes. Zero compromise. Every sneaker and garment passes through our proprietary 4-stage forensic authentication protocol before reaching our shelves.

STAGE 01

UV & Optical Forensics

High-frequency 365nm ultraviolet sweeps detect invisible factory ink stamps, midsole glue leak luminescence, and unauthorized stitching guidelines.

Inspection Spectrum 365nm UV
STAGE 02

Stitch & Pitch Precision

Digital micrometer analysis measuring stitch density per inch, toe-box perforation pitch, and panel overlap tolerance against authentic factory molds.

Tolerance Threshold ±0.1 mm
STAGE 03

Weight & Material Density

Gram-precision scale calibration, sole rubber durometer hardness checks, and factory olfactory profile testing to confirm authentic raw material origins.

Weight Variance < 1.5g Delta
STAGE 04

Tamper-Proof NFC Lock

Upon verification, an encrypted cryptographic NFC tag is permanently locked to the shoe eyelet, tying your pair to an unforgeable digital certificate.

Certification Encrypted NFC
